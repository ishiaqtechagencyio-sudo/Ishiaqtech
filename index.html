IshiaqTech/How we work

From brief to delivery, without surprises.

Every project, from a blog post to a brand film, follows the same four steps. You always know what happens next and what it costs.

Start a project →
  1. 01

    Step 1 · Brief

    Tell us what you need

    Send a message through the form or WhatsApp with your goal, audience, deadline and any files you already have. We reply within one working day with questions and a plan.

    • A short call or chat to clarify the brief
    • A fixed quote and timeline
    • No work starts until you approve
  2. 02

    Step 2 · Concept

    Approve the direction first

    Before full production we show you the direction: an outline for writing, a moodboard or concepts for design, a sitemap and key screens for websites, a script or storyboard for video.

    • Outline, moodboard, wireframe or storyboard
    • Your feedback shapes the plan
    • No surprises later
  3. 03

    Step 3 · Create

    We make it, you stay updated

    We write, design, build or edit, and share progress at each milestone so you can see the work take shape and comment early.

    • Progress updates at each milestone
    • Up to two rounds of revisions
    • Feedback by WhatsApp, email or comments
  4. 04

    Step 4 · Deliver

    Everything ready to use

    You receive the final files in every format you need, plus any source files, so you can publish, print or launch straight away.

    • Final files in every format you need
    • Source files handed over
    • Full rights once the final invoice is paid

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp or use the form below and we will reply within a working day.

Which languages do you translate?

Tell us the language pair in your brief. We confirm availability, the translator and the turnaround in your quote before any work starts.

Can you write in my voice?

Yes. Send two or three things you have written, or we can record a short call. We build a voice guide and use it for every piece.

Who owns the rights to the writing?

You do. Full rights transfer to you once the final invoice is paid, and ghostwritten work is never credited to us.

How long does an ebook take?

It depends on length and how much material you already have. We agree a chapter-by-chapter schedule in the quote.
