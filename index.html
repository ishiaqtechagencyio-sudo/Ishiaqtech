FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Send us a message.

What does IshiaqTech do?

We combine data analytics, business intelligence and digital experience design — so you can understand your business better and present it better online.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We work with businesses and teams of all sizes where data and digital growth can create meaningful value.

Can you work with our existing data and tools?

Usually, yes. We regularly work with Excel, Google Sheets, SQL databases and common business platforms, connecting them into Power BI or custom reports.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A focused dashboard or landing page is typically a matter of weeks; larger BI or website projects are planned in phases with clear milestones.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Absolutely. We work remotely with clients anywhere, using video calls and shared workspaces to keep collaboration smooth.
