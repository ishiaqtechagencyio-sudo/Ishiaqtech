The service

Video is the most persuasive format online, and the most unforgiving. We shape raw footage into stories with rhythm: clear, polished and built for the platform where they will be watched.

Whether we shoot it or you do, the edit is where the story is found. We build structure first, then refine pacing, colour, sound and motion until every second earns its place.

We cut for the destination: widescreen for your site and YouTube, vertical for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, and short teasers for ads. One shoot can become a whole content library.

With our writing and translation team in-house, we add scripts, captions and multilingual subtitles so your video reaches audiences beyond one language.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you shoot or only edit?

Both. You can hire us for full production, or send footage for editing only.

How long does editing take?

Simple social edits move fast, while brand films take longer. We agree a clear timeline up front for every project.

Can you add subtitles in other languages?

Yes. Our translation team prepares timed subtitles in multiple languages.

What formats will I receive?

We export for each platform you need, such as widescreen, vertical and square, in the correct specs.
